# CORELINE Smart Spine Corset

## Adaptive spinal support for work, movement, and everyday life.

CORELINE is an open-development project focused on creating a next-generation spinal support system that combines biomechanics, advanced materials, and future smart integration.

---

## Mission

Our mission is to develop an adaptive spinal support system designed to support posture, help manage spinal load, and encourage more comfortable movement — built transparently and collaboratively.

---

## What Is CORELINE?

CORELINE Smart Corset is being developed as a lightweight, breathable, and modular spinal support system designed to:

- Support lumbar and thoracic alignment
- Help manage strain during prolonged sitting or standing
- Provide posture support during daily activity and movement
- Evolve into a smart wearable platform over time

The system works mechanically first — sensors and software are optional future layers, not dependencies.

## Development Approach

CORELINE is being developed iteratively, with an emphasis on practical mechanical support first.

Current development priorities include:

- Mechanical design and fit
- Wearability and comfort
- Material selection
- Prototype testing and validation
- Modular construction
- Future integration of sensors and software

Smart features are considered a future development layer. The core concept does not depend on electronics to function.
